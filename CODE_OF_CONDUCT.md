# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Expression Lab is an open-source project built with the belief that technology should be welcoming and accessible to everyone, regardless of their experience level, background, or identity.

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and collaborative space for all contributors and users.

---

## Expected Standards

We ask everyone participating in this project (discussions, issues, pull requests) to adhere to a few simple, human principles:

* **Be respectful and kind**: Treat others with empathy. Disagreements happen, but they should always remain constructive and focused on the technical problem, never on the person.
* **Assume good intentions**: Everyone brings different perspectives and skill levels. Approach questions and contributions with patience.
* **No gatekeeping or elitism**: Nobody knows everything. Phrases like *"why didn't you just read the code?"* or dismissive attitudes towards beginners have no place here. Questions and learning are encouraged.
* **Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully**: Keep critiques focused on improving the code or documentation, and be open to learning from each other.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:
* Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory language.
* Trolling, insulting, or belittling comments.
* Any conduct that discriminates against individuals based on identity, background, or experience.

---

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you encounter or experience behavior that violates these principles, please reach out directly:

* **Email**: [github@andersonsalas.com](mailto:github@andersonsalas.com)

All reports will be treated with confidentiality and reviewed promptly to maintain a healthy and welcoming environment for everyone.
